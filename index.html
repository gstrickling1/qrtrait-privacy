QRtrait Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

In short: QRtrait processes your photos entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else — we don't have servers, accounts, or analytics. The only data that leaves your device is standard advertising data collected by Google's ad service (removed entirely if you purchase the Pro upgrade) and purchase processing handled by Google Play.

Who we are

QRtrait is published by Gene Strickling ("we," "us"). Contact: genestrickling@gmail.com.

What QRtrait does with your photos

QRtrait turns a photo you choose — a face, a place, anything — into a personalized, scannable QR code. To do this it needs access to photos you choose, via your photo library or your camera. All processing happens on your device:

  • Face detection (used to frame portraits when your photo contains faces) uses Google ML Kit's on-device face detection. Your photo is analyzed locally and is not sent to Google or anyone else for this purpose.
  • QR code generation and scanning run entirely on your device.
  • Cropping and editing happen entirely on your device.
  • Finished portraits are saved to your own device's gallery, under your control. You can delete them at any time using your gallery app.

We never receive, store, transmit, or sell your photos. QRtrait has no user accounts, no cloud storage, and no analytics or tracking of our own.

Permissions we request

  • Camera — only if you choose to take a new photo inside the app.
  • Photos / media access — to let you pick an existing photo and to save your finished portraits to your gallery.

Both permissions are optional and requested only when you use the corresponding feature.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version of QRtrait shows ads served by Google AdMob. Like most mobile ad services, AdMob may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. This collection is performed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads on Android). Purchasing the Pro upgrade removes all ads, and with them all AdMob data collection.

Purchases

The one-time Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never see your payment information. The app stores only a local record that Pro is unlocked. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google processes payments.

Data we collect

None, directly. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information ourselves. The only third-party data collection is the AdMob advertising data and Google Play purchase processing described above.

Children

QRtrait is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The free version's advertising is provided by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

Data retention and deletion

Everything QRtrait creates — portraits and settings — lives on your device. Deleting the app (and any saved portraits from your gallery) removes everything. There is nothing for us to delete on any server, because we have none.

Changes to this policy

If we change how QRtrait handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
